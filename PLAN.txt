## MISSION: Fix Dashboard to Work with Actual Hugging Face Space API

### UNDERSTANDING THE REAL API

The Space has 12 EXACT feature names:

1. total_building_load (RT) - cooling load in tons
2. avg_chilled_water_rate (L/sec) - chilled water flow rate
3. avg_cooling_water_temp (°C) - cooling water temperature
4. avg_outside_temp (°F) - outside temperature
5. avg_dew_point (°F) - dew point
6. avg_humidity (%) - relative humidity
7. avg_wind_speed (mph) - wind speed
8. avg_pressure (in Hg) - atmospheric pressure
9. hour (0-23) - hour of day
10. day_of_week (0-6, 0=Monday) - day of week
11. month (1-12) - month
12. day_of_year (1-366) - day of year

Plus optional: current_chw_setpoint_c (°C) for optimization
